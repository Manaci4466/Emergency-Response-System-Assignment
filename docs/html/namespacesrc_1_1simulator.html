Detailed Description

Phase 6a: online simulation, one allocation round per incident timestamp.

No clairvoyance: a round at time t only sees the incidents with timestamp == t and the current responder states. Assignments are committed immediately (decision 7); a later, more urgent incident cannot take a responder back, it can only queue behind it. Edge costs are computed ONCE from the t = 0 beliefs (decision 3): road reports are available at t = 0 and the traffic observations are historical, so no new evidence arrives during the simulation.
